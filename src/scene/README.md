# Scene layer

`WarehouseScene.tsx` owns the optional React integration. `createWarehouseScene.ts` owns renderer, cameras, picking, navigation, postprocessing, export and cleanup. `buildWarehouseWorld.ts` builds the procedural facility from validated config and records. Business transitions remain in the domain/server.

World units are meters, Y up. The rectangular floor is centered at X/Z zero. Warehouse floor is Y=0; yard surface is approximately Y=-0.59. Rack bays are 2.4 m long, rack depth 1.2 m, level pitch 1.25 m, with two rack faces surrounding the configured aisle width. Storage addresses map to exact rack/bin coordinates generated by the domain.

Shared box/rounded-box/cylinder/sphere primitives construct steel frames, cross bracing, decking, pallet boards/feet, cartons, scanner, pack benches, rollers, operators, trucks, dock frames, roof/trusses and architectural fixtures. Procedural seeded canvas textures provide concrete/cardboard grain, tape, barcodes and address plaques. Plaques use cached 256px textures. Stock and pallet parts are instanced; fixed meshes are merged by material, parent and location ID. Each picked instance resolves to its stable address.

Dock poses come from `domain/facility.ts`. They lie on the exterior perimeter for all templates. A vehicle's outward local axis is -Z; its rear aligns just outside the threshold, cab remains in the yard, and vertical offset aligns trailer floor with the warehouse slab. Only docked/held truck records render vehicles; builder preview is explicitly illustrative.

Room-environment PBR lighting, ACES tone mapping, directional shadows, fill light and half-resolution SSAO provide depth. Renderer pixel ratio is capped. Main rendering runs at 30 FPS. Dashboard thumbnails use SVG rather than WebGL. Window/document visibility prevents drawing when hidden. Cameras support orbit, top view and first-person 1.72m eye height, smooth focus flights, and guarded walking routes. Route segments are sampled for clearance, not merely endpoint-tested.

Cutaway keeps the near shell visible with transparent material variants and hides the roof. Complete-building restores opaque walls and roof. Walking has a solid shell. Transparent shell meshes do not intercept interior picking. GLB export temporarily restores the whole building and excludes selection highlights.

On replacement/unmount, cancel frames, disconnect resize observers/listeners, dispose controls, postprocessing targets, environment, geometries, materials, textures and renderer, then release the WebGL context. Failure provides a real generated SVG plan and preserves the record directory. Scene animation never writes inventory or implies live tracking of illustrative staff/equipment.
